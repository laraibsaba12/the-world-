Badshahi Mosque

The Badshahi Mosque is a historic and grand mosque located in Lahore. It was built during the reign of the Mughal Emperor Aurangzeb. It is one of the largest mosques in the world and is famous for its beautiful architecture, red sandstone structure, and vast courtyard. Thousands of people visit it daily for prayer and tourism.

Faisal Mosque

Faisal Mosque is located at the foothills of the Margalla Hills in Islamaba d and is the largest mosque in Pakistan. Its unique design looks like a desert tent instead of traditional domes. It was named after King Faisal of Saudi Arabia and is considered a national symbol of Pakistan.

Minar-e-Pakistan

Minar-e-Pakistan is an important national monument in Lahore. It marks the place where the Pakistan Resolution was passed in 1940. The monument represents the struggle for independence and attracts many visitors every year. It is surrounded by a beautiful park, making it a popular tourist spot.
